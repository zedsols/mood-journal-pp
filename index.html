Hunz

A quick, private mood journal. Log how you feel in a tap, add a short note, and see your patterns over time.

What you can do

  • Log your mood in a tap, with an optional short note and tags.
  • Look back through your history and see insights about your moods.
  • Set reminders and log straight from a home-screen widget.
  • Use it fully offline. No account needed.

Your data stays yours

Your entries are stored on your phone. Hunz has no account of its own, and we run no servers that hold your journal.

Optional Google Drive backup. If you turn it on, Hunz signs in with your Google account and saves a copy of your entries, notes and tags to a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so a new phone can pick up where this one left off. Hunz can see only the files it creates in that folder, and nothing else in your Drive. You can disconnect at any time.
