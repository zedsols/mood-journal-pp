Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Hunz ("the App") is a mood and journaling application. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Storage

All data you enter in the App, including mood entries, notes, tags and settings, is stored locally on your device. The App has no account of its own, and we do not operate any server that receives or stores your journal data. Your journal data leaves your device only if you turn on Google Drive backup (below) or export it yourself.

Google Drive Backup (optional)

The App offers an optional backup to your own Google Drive. It is off until you choose "Connect Google Drive" in the App's Settings.

  • What we access. When you connect, you sign in with Google and grant the App one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This lets the App create, read, update and delete only its own files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. The App cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. From your Google account, the App also reads your name and email address, which it shows on the Backup screen so you know which account is connected.
  • What is backed up. A copy of your mood entries, notes, tags and the links between them, plus the time of the backup and the number of entries. Your settings and reminders are not backed up.
  • How we use it. The backup is used only to restore your entries, on this phone or a new one, when you choose to restore. It is sent directly from your device to Google; it does not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot read it.
  • Where it is stored. In your own Google Drive, under your Google account, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. It counts toward your Google storage.
  • Sharing. We do not sell, share or transfer data obtained from Google to anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • Stopping and deleting. "Disconnect Google Drive" in the App stops new backups and signs the App out of your Google account on that device; the existing copy stays in your Drive. "Delete all my data" while connected also empties the Drive backup. To remove the backup from your Drive entirely, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Hunz and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove the App's access at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps & services).

Hunz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Troubleshooting Data

The App uses a third-party crash reporting and diagnostics service (Sentry) to help us find and fix bugs. When the App crashes, encounters an error, or (for a small sample of sessions) as part of normal use, this service may collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, app logs, and a recording of on-screen activity at the time of the issue, so we can reproduce and fix the problem. Along with each report the service receives your device's IP address, as any internet request does. Screen recordings mask on-screen text and images, so they show the layout of a screen, not what you wrote. This diagnostic data does not include your journal entries, notes, tags, or your Google account details. It is sent to the service provider's servers, used only to diagnose and fix problems, and is not sold or used for advertising.

Notifications

The App may send you local reminder notifications. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App's Settings.

Widget

The App provides a home screen widget that reads data stored on your device. No journal data leaves your device to display widget content.

App Updates

The App checks for and downloads small app updates from Expo's update service (expo.dev). These requests carry only technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, platform and your device's IP address. No journal data is sent.

Ratings

The App may occasionally ask whether you would like to rate it, and Settings has a "Rate Hunz" row. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms; the App does not see or receive what you submit.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the crash reporting and diagnostics service and the app update service described above, Google Play for ratings, and, only if you turn on backup, Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. It does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or ad-tracking services.

Data Export and Import

The App offers an optional data export feature that saves your entries to a file on your device. This file is not uploaded anywhere automatically. You choose where to save or share it.

The App can also import entries from an export file you choose. The file is read on your device and its entries are added to your local journal; nothing is uploaded.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete all your entries from the App's Settings ("Delete all my data"). Uninstalling the App removes the data stored on your device. A Google Drive backup, if you made one, is removed as described under Google Drive Backup.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. We never receive your journal data, and diagnostic data collected by the crash reporting service is limited to technical troubleshooting information, not content you write.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date above will change.
